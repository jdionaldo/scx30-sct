# Reference study: Orlandoo-Hunter OH32X02 (1/32 SCT, X-Roll Cage kit)

Source: `/Users/johndionaldo/Downloads/OH32X02.pdf` (32-page assembly manual, read as page images). Tags: CONFIRMED = printed in the manual; INFERRED = read from part numbers or drawings; the manual has no tolerances or material data. Not a design spec for our truck: it is a 1/32 kit with moulded parts and photo-etched steel.

## Size (CONFIRMED, manual p.1)
168 L x 79 W x 59 H mm, wheelbase 104, track 67. Ours: wheelbase 116.5. Motor: 12x10x9 mm geared unit, 2.2 g servo, 2S LiPo.

## Cage / chassis (steps 4-7, 11-14, 26)
- The cage is the chassis: one-piece space frame (OHPCD00002), triangulated into regular bays, tubes meeting at round node pads with screw bosses.
- Photo-etched steel triangles (19 pieces, M1.0 screws, bent at one edge) screw onto the node pads as shear panels and floor. This gives torsional stiffness without more tubes. A separate printed tub/box (motor, ESC, battery) is screwed inside at the centre (steps 25-26).
- Front nose and tail are open hoops that slope down toward the ends; the spare tyres and fuel can ride on the rear section, above the rear axle (steps 32-33).
- Body: 4 rubber grommet posts (OHPJB45280), no tilt hinge.

## Front suspension (steps 12-24)
- Independent double wishbone: upper and lower A-arms, steel hinge pins (OHMLM11025, 4x, 25 mm long) with shims for side play.
- Steering knuckles on 3x6 bearings (OHBG30620) with ball studs for the links.
- Servo at the front, steering bar (OHPCF00004) to a rod with adjustable ball ends, tie rods 5 mm and 0 mm between ball-end faces (adjustable turnbuckles).
- Per side a plain damper (7.5 mm set length) plus a sprung shock (17.5 mm), i.e. dual shocks per corner (trophy-truck style).

## Rear suspension (steps 27-31)
- Live axle: two printed housings with bearings (2x5 and 3x6), steel axle shaft, bevel gear, driven by a two-piece universal-joint driveshaft (OHPCC52001).
- Long flat trailing arms (OHPCF00003, 57 mm, left and right) with the shocks standing nearly vertical on the arm mid-span; arrows in the manual show the mounting direction.
- Four adjustable rods: two upper links (set 29 mm) and two more (set 0 / 10 mm), all ball-ended, adjustable by twisting.
- Rear shock pair per side: plain damper (12 mm) plus sprung shock (28.5 mm) with two springs in series (soft and firm) and a collar, so a progressive rate.
- Driveshaft inclination aimed at about 5 degrees with the axle at rest (manual p.31 text); adjust with the upper-rod length.

## Hardware (INFERRED from part numbers)
Mostly tiny screws: OHSW01002 (49, M1.0 x 2), 01203/01204 (M1.2 x 3/4), 01005 (M1.0 x 5), 01503, 11603. Ball studs (OHMLA20162) with plastic ball-end sockets on every link. One tool kit: Phillips, nut driver, L hex key.

## Ideas to consider for our 1/30 build
1. Cage: keep to regular bays with one diagonal each; thin printed gusset panels (about 0.8-1.0 mm) on the node pads could replace extra tubes for stiffness.
2. Rear shock: nearly vertical, on the arm mid-span (lever ratio about 0.5-0.6), instead of our 28.6 degree lean.
3. Dual-rate or two-spring stack on the rear shock for progressive travel.
4. Adjustable ball-end links (turnbuckles) on the upper links and steering: lets the owner tune axle alignment and anti-squat without reprinting.
5. Steel hinge pins with shims for arm side play.
6. Ballast and CG advice from the manual: add weight low and equal left and right.
