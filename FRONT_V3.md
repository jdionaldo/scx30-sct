## Why the servo is upside-down
The servo is 20.13 mm tall; the hood is only Z ≈ 15.5–17 over the axle and everything under it (worm Z 5–8.5, ring gear, arms ≤ 8.6) is occupied. So the case sits **upside-down above the arms/worm**, output shaft pointing down to a low horn that drives the v2 rack (scotch yoke).
