## UPDATE 8 — axle chain per the exploded Injora/stock view (all dimensions ESTIMATES, named constants in `13_front_v3.py`)
Chain (front): diff outputs (slotted axle shaft) → **dogbone with a joint at BOTH ends** → stub axle through the knuckle (2 bearings, back to back) → hex hub. Rear = plain axle shafts + bearings (no CVD).
Model changes: second stub-axle bearing added (`Front_Bearing_Inner_L/R`, Ø4×1.6, in the hub sleeve; the original sits in the wall); constants `DOG_J1_Y` (diff-side joint, 10.3), `DOG_J2_DY` (knuckle joint offset along the stub axle from the kingpin axis, 0), `DOG_SLOT` (slot engagement, 2.5), `BRG_IN_Y/BRG_OUT_Y` (40.5/42.1). The single-joint CVD mesh is still the visual stand-in for the dogbone; the real stock dogbone replaces it (no printed part).
**Per-end angles** (lock ±2.8 mm, arm −12..+4 and −12..+12; printed by the script's DOGBONE block):
| case | max diff-end | max knuckle-end | plunge |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1 10.3, J2 on kingpin (default) | 10.2° | **36.7°** (full lock, arm 0) | −0.10..+0.01 mm |
| J2 offset +2.0 along stub axle | 9.8° | 34.2° | −0.43..+0.01 |
| J1 at 14 / 18 (shorter dogbone) | 11.9° / 14.5° | 36.7° | ~0 |
Reading: the diff end is trivial (≤15° even for short dogbones). The knuckle end equals the wheel steer angle (36.7° inner wheel at ±2.8 mm), so a 30–35° stock joint is marginal/over at full lock. Options: (a) stock dogbone and limit lock to ≈2.4 mm (≤~32°), (b) keep ±2.8 with a ≥40° joint, (c) place the knuckle joint ~2 mm outboard of the kingpin axis (34.2°) — slightly under 35° but not a margin. Hub travel barely matters (diff-end ≤0.1° change from bump).
Plunge is essentially nil with the joint on the kingpin axis, so slot engagement (`DOG_SLOT`, capacity ≈ ±2.0 mm) is not the limit; the limit is the joint angle. Joint centre must sit on the kingpin axis (DOG_J2_DY≈0) or plunge/steer scrub grows (0.43 mm at +2).
Fasteners to add to the BOM (from the view): 2× M1.4×2 set screws per hub/axle, 2× bearing-retainer screws per knuckle M1.0×4 (stock) / M1.0×2.5 (Injora), diff cover M1.0×6 ×2.
Sweeps rerun: contact matrix identical to before (droop beyond −8° at full lock still touches spindle/arm, pre-existing), 15 printed parts watertight after STL roundtrip, bulkhead floor holes unchanged, no tub files touched.
