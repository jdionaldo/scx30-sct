Generated by `scripts/07_front_v2.py` (manifold3d booleans + python-fcl collision sweeps).
Parts: `stl/v2/*.stl` (all watertight). `scx30_sct_107mm_kit.sc` now carries the v2 front end (91 bodies; only the intentional CVD-half overlap remains). The JSON scripts cannot rebuild the whole `.sc` faithfully (the body shell differs even at the previous commit): treat the `.sc` as the source of truth.

## Verified (script output)
* All 13 new/changed printed parts: watertight, positive volume.
* Rest pose: no real interference (only intentional insertions: CVD into spool, CVD halves, pins in bores).
* **Steering:** ±2.75 mm rack → inner/outer wheel **35° / 25°** (proper Ackermann), servo horn ±32°. Lock is free of contact.
* **Bump steer** (rack centred): toe −0.16° at ride, −0.6° at +8°, −2° at +15° (acceptable up to usable travel).
* **CVD plunge** needed over usable travel: < 0.05 mm (fixed-length dogbone is fine).
* **Usable front travel (first contact):** droop to arm −8° (hub −1.5 mm from ride), bump to +8° (hub +4.6 mm).
  Droop stop = lower arm on bulkhead; bump = tie rod/spindle and, with full lock, tyre on body shell (limit +2 mm at full lock).
  → total ≈ 6 mm wheel travel. Use bump stops; do not run shocks past this.

## NOT verified / limits (be aware)
* Gearbox (B0HMR5D54N) and motor are not modelled. `Ref_Gearbox_Envelope` (X ±9, Y ±9.6, Z −1.9…8) is only the **free space** between rails/skid/cage — check your real gearbox fits it. The shaft assumes a front output at **Z 6.85** (mirror of rear output is Z 5.5; adjust with a short U-joint or ≤3° angle).
* Front is a **locked spool** (single cross-shaft ring gear) — expect understeer; a diff is not modelled.
* **Rear travel (fixed, `08_relief.py`/`09_verify_rear.py`):** the axle housing used to clear the rails by <1 mm (≈0 bump). `Rear_Sway_Bridge` is now raised 10 mm and the rail tips + shell arches are relieved. Verified free from −10° (−9.7 mm droop) to +6° (+5.1 mm bump); first contact at +7° (rail/axle, arm/shell). Fit bump stops at ≈ +5 mm. The sway-bar line moves up 10 mm: re-check drop-link length.
* **Shell arches (fixed):** wheel envelope (arm −8…+8°, rack ±2.75 mm) is cut from `Tacoma_Trophy_Body_Shell` (+0.5 mm clearance); `10_verify_front_shell.py` finds no wheel/shell contact. Shell stays watertight, 1 shell.
* Thin parts (print PA-CF/PETG-CF, 0.1 mm layers, 100 % infill): rack 0.9 mm, horn 0.9, tie rods 1.1 (or use Ø1.5 wire), spindle arm 1.1.
* **Servo dims are unverified and probably wrong** (see `GEARBOX_FIT.md`: modelled 6×12 mm body vs ~21–24×15 mm real SCX30 servos). Gearbox dims could not be found online. Measure both with calipers and change `SERVO_*` / the gearbox envelope in `07_front_v2.py` before printing the servo mount.
